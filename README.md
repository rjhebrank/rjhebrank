### Projects

**[Kalshi Asian Binary Pricing](https://github.com/rjhebrank/kalshi-asian-binary-pricing)**
Kalshi settles its Bitcoin contracts on a 60-second average, not a closing print,
which makes them Asian binaries rather than the vanilla binaries most
participants price. This is the Turnbull-Wakeman pricer that corrects for it,
with a 30-test suite and results from six weeks of trading it with real money.

More to come. Other projects are in progress and will appear here as they are
